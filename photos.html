How to add a picture + caption

1. Put the image inside assets/photos/. For example: assets/photos/student-government.jpg

2. In photos.html, replace one placeholder block with:

<figure class="photo-slot">
  <img src="assets/photos/student-government.jpg" alt="Student Government event">
  <figcaption>Planning an LTU campus event with Student Government.</figcaption>
</figure>

3. Change the words inside <figcaption>...</figcaption> to whatever short caption you want. The styling is already built in.

Tip: You can keep adding <figure> blocks. The gallery will automatically arrange them into the cute collage layout.
