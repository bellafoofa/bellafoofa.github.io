CASE_008 // IMAGE PROCESSING

MEDICAL IMAGING + FOURIER FILTERING

Coursework exploring how spatial-frequency filtering changes medical images and how those changes should be interpreted.

CASE NOTES_

LOOK AT THE IMAGE DIFFERENTLY

I enjoy the combination of visual reasoning, quantitative analysis, and troubleshooting that image processing requires.

  • Worked with Fourier transforms and frequency-domain filtering.
  • Compared original, transformed, filtered, and reconstructed images.
  • Documented filter choices and visual effects.
  • Connected image-processing steps to noise and feature visibility.
WHY IT MATTERS_

This case file focuses on the engineering decisions, troubleshooting, testing, and iteration behind the work—not just the final result.
