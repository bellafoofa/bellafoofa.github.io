CASE_003 // MEDICAL DEVICE DESIGN

PPA LOAD CELL

A pressure-sensing mobility-aid concept built around load-cell measurement, feedback, and connected rehabilitation monitoring.

CASE NOTES_

SENSE → INTERPRET → FEEDBACK

The strongest part of this project is the connection between physical sensing and a practical rehabilitation use case.

  • Integrated sensing into a mobility-aid tip concept.
  • Used load-cell data as the basis for feedback and tracking.
  • Focused on useful feedback without making the device harder to use.
  • Applied verification and iteration thinking to the sensing workflow.
WHY IT MATTERS_

This case file focuses on the engineering decisions, troubleshooting, testing, and iteration behind the work—not just the final result.
