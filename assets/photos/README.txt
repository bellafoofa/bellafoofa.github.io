ADD YOUR PERSONAL PHOTOS HERE

1. Put lab, Student Government, volunteering, campus, friends, or event photos in this folder.
2. Then edit photos.html and replace a photo-placeholder with an <img> inside the matching <figure class="photo-slot">.
3. Put a short caption in <figcaption>...</figcaption>.

Example:
<figure class="photo-slot">
  <img src="assets/photos/my-lab-day.jpg" alt="Working in the biomedical engineering lab">
  <figcaption>Working on a biomedical engineering lab project.</figcaption>
</figure>
