CASE_010 // WEARABLE DESIGN

NUITLUME

An illuminated knee-brace concept designed around visibility, safety, and hands-free use.

CASE NOTES_

WEARABLE, BUT PRACTICAL

The goal was to add useful visibility without turning the brace into a bulky electronics project.

  • Integrated lighting into a wearable form factor.
  • Considered comfort, placement, battery access, and visibility.
  • Explored consumer, medical, and safety use cases.
  • Used iterative sketches and component planning to refine the concept.
WHY IT MATTERS_

This case file focuses on the engineering decisions, troubleshooting, testing, and iteration behind the work—not just the final result.
