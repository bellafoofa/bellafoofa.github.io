CASE_009 // HUMAN MOVEMENT

BIOMECHANICS MOTION ANALYSIS

Motion-analysis work using tracked body points and center-of-gravity behavior to interpret movement.

CASE NOTES_

FROM VIDEO TO MEASUREMENT

This work reinforced how much useful engineering information can come from careful tracking, visualization, and interpretation.

  • Tracked body landmarks through jump movement.
  • Compared static squat-jump and countermovement-jump patterns.
  • Used position-versus-time plots to interpret COG motion.
  • Connected visual movement patterns to biomechanical reasoning.
WHY IT MATTERS_

This case file focuses on the engineering decisions, troubleshooting, testing, and iteration behind the work—not just the final result.
