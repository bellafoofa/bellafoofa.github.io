In progress · 2026–2027

Mopec Medical AI Project

As part of LTU’s BME company-sponsored senior project, I’m working on a team developing, training, and fine-tuning an AI model for Mopec with the goal of supporting future use in hospitals, medical facilities, and related healthcare environments.

The project is still in its early stages, so the current work is centered on building a strong machine-learning workflow: understanding the application, reviewing available datasets, planning training/validation/testing, and establishing a clear path for model development and evaluation.
