Mopec Medical AI Senior Project

I’m part of LTU’s BME company-sponsored senior project with Mopec. Our team is developing, training, and fine-tuning an AI model intended for future use in healthcare environments such as hospitals, medical facilities, and related pathology workflows.

Because the project is still early, the current work is centered on building a strong foundation: reviewing available datasets, shaping the training / validation / test approach, and organizing a workflow that can support future model evaluation and iteration.
